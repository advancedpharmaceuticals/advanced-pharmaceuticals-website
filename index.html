WHO WE ARE

Focused on quality.
Built on reliability.

Advanced Pharmaceuticals works across pharmaceutical manufacturing and distribution, with a portfolio spanning multiple therapeutic categories. We aim to support dependable access to medicines through careful operations, quality-conscious processes and strong relationships with pharmacy partners.

Our manufacturing hub is located in Rawat, while our Islamabad operations support coordination and distribution.
